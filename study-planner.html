Backfill / Catch-up Log

Log classes taught before you started using this app — whether that's a few missed lectures or joining mid-year with months of school already behind you. Backfilled chapters are marked taught immediately, but won't all dump into your revision queue at once — they roll in gradually over the following days instead of showing up as one big overdue pile. Revision scheduling only begins once you actively self-study or revise a chapter (via "Mark Done" or the revision flow) — backfill alone does not create revision tasks.
